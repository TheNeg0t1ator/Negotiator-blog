# Welcome to Iter8

Hi there! Welcome to **Iter8**, my blog where I dive into the fascinating world of continuous improvement and the power of iteration. I chose the name **Iter8** because it stems from the word **Iteration**, which represents a method of development that focuses on constant enhancement and solving problems step-by-step. The "8" in the name stands for **Infinity** \\((\infty) \\), highlighting the limitless potential for growth and innovation.



## Why Iteration?

Iteration is at the heart of everything I do. By iterating, we can:

- **Learn from mistakes**: Each iteration provides valuable feedback, helping us avoid previous pitfalls and build on our successes.
- **Adapt to change**: Iterative processes are flexible and responsive, allowing us to adapt to new information and changing circumstances.
